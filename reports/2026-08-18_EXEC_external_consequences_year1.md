# H-ACS — Year-1 EXEC: External Consequences Register

## Purpose

This report formalizes the EXEC layer discussed during the second-order audit of H-ACS.

The objective is to distinguish observable changes in reality from META material: interpretations, hypotheses, symbolic readings, and methodological narratives.

> **Core EXEC filter: What changed in reality that can be shown without telling the story about ourselves?**

## META vs EXEC

### META

What was understood, hypothesized, interpreted, or formulated.

### EXEC

What changed in observable reality as a result or correlate of the work.

EXEC is already present in Year 1. It is not deferred until Year 2. However, Year 1 is predominantly a longitudinal META/development phase, so not every session is expected to produce an EXEC consequence.

## Year-1 Observable Consequences

### 1. SATURN Switch

A concrete behavioral mechanism was independently developed within H-ACS and subsequently used during a real interpersonal/professional conflict.

Observable consequence:

`TRIGGER → SATURN → STOP → SILENCE → ANALYSIS → STRUCTURE → RESPONSE`

Evidence status: **Operationally Tested**.

### 2. Oath continuity

The creator maintained the declared Oath / fast-dopamine abstinence architecture across a prolonged period of real-life load.

The archive records continuity of the behavioral restrictions rather than merely a plan to maintain them.

Evidence status: **Observed Longitudinal Result**.

### 3. External memory infrastructure

The methodology moved beyond transient chat context into persistent external storage including Moleskine, dated session archives, Cycle Table records, and repository artifacts.

Observable consequence:

`conversation → persistent record → retrievable longitudinal dataset`

Evidence status: **Operationally Implemented**.

### 4. Public digital infrastructure

The H-ACS project established public GitHub repositories and public social-media traces.

Observable consequence:

`private cognitive work → externally addressable digital artifacts`

This is evidence of externalization of the project, but not evidence of external adoption or market validation.

Evidence status: **Externalized**.

### 5. Professional transition

The creator's employment relationship with BelTНПП ended in July 2026, followed by an active search for new professional opportunities and work on B2B consulting positioning.

This is a real external change in the creator's professional environment, but it is not itself evidence that H-ACS caused the change.

Evidence status: **Observed External Event**.

### 6. Delivery of material outside the private archive

The creator completed and transmitted at least one substantive professional/public document outside the private H-ACS conversation environment.

This marks a real transition from internal documentation to external delivery.

Evidence status: **Externalized**.

## What EXEC Does Not Prove

The current Year-1 EXEC record does not establish:

- that H-ACS is universally effective;
- that H-ACS is commercially validated;
- that H-ACS independently caused every listed external outcome;
- that other operators would reproduce the same results;
- that the methodology is ready for broad public deployment.

## Year-1 Interpretation

The correct first-year conclusion is therefore narrow:

> **H-ACS has already produced observable external and behavioral consequences for its first experimental operator, while its generalizability and commercial validity remain untested.**

This is stronger than saying that H-ACS has produced only reflection, but deliberately weaker than claiming that the methodology has been proven.

## Required Evidence Format Going Forward

For every significant H-ACS claim:

`CLAIM → SOURCE → OBSERVABLE CONSEQUENCE → EVIDENCE STATUS`

Where causal attribution is uncertain, record the event without assigning causation.

## Year-1 / Year-2 Boundary

Year 1:

`PRESERVE → OBSERVE → ANALYSE → HYPOTHESIZE → CROSS-CHECK`

with EXEC recorded wherever observable consequences occur.

Year 2:

`EVIDENCE → STABLE PATTERN → PROTOCOL → OPERATIONAL TEST → EXTERNAL REPRODUCIBILITY`

The existence of EXEC in Year 1 does not mean that the project has entered the Year-2 formalization phase.
