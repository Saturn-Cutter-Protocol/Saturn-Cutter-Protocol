## Artifact 021

Title

H-ACS Status: Operationally Tested on One Operator

Observation

During Year 1, H-ACS has been continuously used by one operator — its creator. The longitudinal record contains sustained Oath continuity, SATURN Switch use, external fixation, GitHub activity, Moleskine processing, and observable changes in behaviour and decisions. No independent external operator has yet reproduced the methodology.

Finding

H-ACS has local operational results, but universal reproducibility has not been established.

Conclusion

Evidence status: Operationally Tested — one operator. H-ACS must not be presented as a universally validated methodology before external testing.

## Artifact 024

Title

Documentation Load / Architect Addiction

Observation

As H-ACS expanded, the system designed to reduce cognitive load began generating additional meta-work: documentation, reductions, protocols, and repeated repackaging of substantially similar material.

Finding

Increased methodological volume is not evidence of increased system effectiveness.

Conclusion

New documents and protocols are not results by themselves. Priority remains testing already existing mechanisms and reducing parasitic meta-work. Documentation must not substitute for external life or execution.

## Artifact 025

Title

External Calibration Gap

Observation

H-ACS remains a first-operator experiment. No independent external operator has reproduced the methodology. The creator is simultaneously the subject, principal analyst, and primary judge of the results.

Finding

Absence of an external operator does not demonstrate that H-ACS is ineffective, but it limits the strength of claims about reproducibility and generalization.

Conclusion

Until controlled external testing begins, claims about general validity remain limited to the first-operator longitudinal evidence.

## Artifact 028

Title

Recovery Window After Operational Overload

Observation

Following prolonged intensive operational management, a continuous recovery period may emerge in which the need for complex tasks and even regular AI sessions temporarily decreases. After recovery, work can resume naturally without compensatory overactivity.

Finding

Recovery after prolonged load is not automatically a deviation from discipline.

Conclusion

Recovery Window is recognized as an admissible phase of the operational cycle. Artificially filling recovery periods with additional activity may increase cognitive debt.

## Artifact 029

Title

Self-Reported KPI Integrity Failure

Observation

Earlier engineering documentation used numerical KPIs such as Focus Ratio, Completion Rate, and Recovery Index, while the measurement mechanism relied primarily on self-registration in personal logs. Direct repository audit did not identify an independent validation mechanism for those values. Multiple documents also repackaged substantially the same model in different registers.

Finding

Formal KPI structure existed before a sufficiently independent measurement mechanism.

Conclusion

Self-reported numerical KPIs must not be treated as evidence of system effectiveness without a defined measurement procedure. Future metrics require a clear variable definition, measurement method, frequency, and verification path.
