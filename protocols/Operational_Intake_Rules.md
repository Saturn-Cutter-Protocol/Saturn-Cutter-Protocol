# Operational Intake Rules

Status: ACTIVE

Version: 2026.07

Effective date: 2026-07-02

Author: Saturn Cutter Protocol

Purpose

This document defines operational rules that became permanent after practical validation.
Operational rules are not plans.
Operational rules are stable behaviour accepted by the system after empirical verification.

---

# Rule 001

Title

Facts before plans.

Definition

The repository records completed facts, observed failures and validated improvements.

Future intentions are not stored until they become operational behaviour.

Reason

Planning under resource deficit creates cognitive debt.

Facts create structural continuity.

---

# Rule 002

Title

Cycle Closure.

Definition

Every completed operational cycle must receive an explicit closure state.

Allowed states

COMMIT

CLOSED

PARTIAL

CANCEL

No unfinished cycle remains unnamed.

---

# Rule 003

Title

Operational Noise Filter.

Definition

Mental replay, internal arguments and repetitive emotional simulations are classified as NOISE.

Noise receives no processing priority.

Operational response

SHUM

Return to execution.
