# Purpose

The SATURN Cognitive Firewall is the primary interrupt mechanism of the H-ACS architecture.

Its purpose is to suspend automatic emotional execution, isolate external stimuli from immediate behavioral output, and restore deliberate Agency before any decision is made.

This protocol is not intended to suppress emotions.

It creates a controlled execution gap between stimulus and response.

# Core Premise

Agency is the ability to impose structure upon chaos.

# SATURN Execution Protocol

## Trigger

Detection of one or more of the following:

- emotional provocation
- manipulation
- office conflict
- psychological pressure
- chaotic request
- urgency imposed by another person
- interruption of planned work
- cognitive overload

---

## Step 1 — Hardware Switch

Internal command:

```
[SATURN]
```

This command immediately interrupts the Legacy System.

---

## Step 2 — Physical Freeze

Duration:

Approximately three seconds.

Rules:

- silence
- no explanation
- no justification
- no defensive behavior
- no emotional output

The objective is to prevent automatic execution.

---

## Step 3 — Structural Analysis

Discard:

- emotional payload
- accusations
- urgency
- manipulation
- tone

Extract only:

- objective facts
- operational requirements
- system relevance

---

## Step 4 — Agency Restoration

Ask internally:

> What action increases structural stability?

Not:

> How should I emotionally respond?

---

## Step 5 — Controlled Output

Respond only through structure.

Possible outputs include:

- formal technical task
- documented request
- scheduled meeting
- written clarification
- predefined operational process

Never respond to emotional pressure.

Always respond through architecture.
